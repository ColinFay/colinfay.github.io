# Short version

Colin Fay is a lead developer at ThinkR, a French agency focused on everything
R-related. He builds R packages, {shiny} apps that survive production, and
maintains the golemverse.

# Long version

Colin Fay is a lead developer at ThinkR, a French agency focused on everything
R-related.

By day, he helps companies take full advantage of the power of R. He builds
tools, deploys infrastructure, and patiently explains that a {shiny} app is a
serious piece of software like any other. His playground: data & software
engineering, web applications (frontend and backend), and the long unglamorous
road that takes R code all the way to production.

By night, he is an active open source developer and advocate: main developer of
{golem} and the wider golemverse, lead author of "Engineering Production-Grade
Shiny Apps", and he travels the world to complain about computers in front of
an audience.

# Unofficial version (for the brave)

Colin writes R for a living and runs absurdly long distances for fun. He eats
only plants, listens to music that sounds like a chainsaw falling down a flight
of stairs, and holds strong opinions about putting R in production. Buy him a
coffee, and he will tell you about all four, whether you asked or not.
